Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At CHAI & CO., accessible from our stalls and digital portals, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information collected and recorded by CHAI & CO. and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for Chai Rewards, place a demo order, or submit a catering request, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and tea flavor preferences to provide personalized service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to communicate rewards point balance, process catering bookings, provide customer support, and send optional newsletters regarding seasonal tea flushes.

3. Cookies

Like any modern web application, CHAI & CO. uses cookies and local storage to store visitor preferences, including light/dark theme settings and shopping cart items.

4. Contact Us

If you have additional questions regarding our privacy practices, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@chaiandco.in.
