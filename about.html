Rooted in Authenticity

Brewed with Tradition. Served with Soul.

CHAI & CO. began in a quiet sunlit lane in Jaipur with a single brass pot, a mortar and pestle, and an unapologetic obsession with brewing authentic, unhurried chai. In an era dominated by artificial concentrates and powdered syrups, we believed people craved something profoundly real: the rhythm of slow rolling bubbles, aromatic crushed pods, and warmth that sinks deep into the spirit.

Over eight years, our humble corner stall transformed into a contemporary sanctuary of tea culture. Today, we partner directly with over 45 organic family-run estates across Assam, Darjeeling, and Kerala to bring single-origin orthodox leaves directly from soil to saucer.

"We don't brew tea merely to fill a cup; we brew it to pause time, nourish human connection, and honor generations of Indian tea craftsmanship."

Aarav Sharma — Founder & Master Tea Sommelier

Our Mission

To bring authentic, memorable chai rituals to everyday modern life without compromise. We preserve heritage Indian recipes while creating an inclusive, beautiful gathering space where every conversation is nourished by real ingredients.

Our Vision

To become the world's most cherished destination celebrating Indian tea craftsmanship. We champion fair living wages for tea pickers, eliminate single-use plastics, and foster warm human connections over steaming clay cups.

Guiding Principles

The Values That Shape Every Pot

Our standard for every leaf plucked, every spice ground, and every customer welcomed.

Uncompromising Authenticity

We never cut corners with tea bags, syrups, or artificial flavour drops. Genuine whole spices only.

Fair Farmer Partnerships

We purchase directly from certified estates, ensuring 20-35% above fair-trade floor prices for estate workers.

Radical Hospitality

Inspired by "Atithi Devo Bhava" (Guest is divine), our stalls and cafes are sanctuaries of warmth and generosity.

Sustainable Rituals

Traditional unglazed terracotta kulhad cups return to the earth naturally. 100% compostable packaging.

Community First

Chai stalls have historically been the town squares of India. We keep that communal spirit alive today.

Artisanal Mastery

Our chai makers undergo a rigorous 4-month culinary training in heat control, spice ratios, and aeration.
