The Art of the 5-Minute Simmer

Great chai is never rushed. Our lounge baristas calibrate boiling times, water mineralization, and milk density to unleash the full aromatic bouquet of volatile spice oils.

Rolling Decoction at 98°C Extracts deep malty tannins from Assam CTC before milk infusion.
Stone-Ground Pods on Order Cardamom and cloves are crushed seconds before boiling to preserve essential cineole aromatics.
Unbaked Terracotta Aeration Porous red clay imparts earthy minerals while cooling the tea to perfect sipping temperature.
