Our Heritage

More Than Tea. It's a Tradition.

CHAI & CO. was founded with a quiet promise: to rescue chai from syrups and artificial powders, returning it to the slow, soul-stirring ritual that has brought communities together across India for centuries.

From the bustling roadside stalls of Kolkata to the serene misty hills of Darjeeling, we source each leaf and spice with deep ethical respect. When you sit in our tea house, you share in a living heritage.
