Heritage Brewing Recipes & Blending Secrets

The authentic proportions, rolling simmer temperatures, and artisanal spice pounding techniques behind our most cherished chai and kaapi creations.

Master Recipe 01 6 Mins Simmer

The 2:1 Golden Degchi Masala Chai

The foundational copper vessel recipe balancing Upper Assam CTC, freshly cracked Idukki green cardamom, and bruised ginger root.

Master Blend Elements:
  • 2 cups Fresh filtered spring water
  • 1 cup Full-fat dairy or creamy oat milk
  • 2 tbsp High-grade Upper Assam CTC leaves
  • 4 pods Green cardamom, cracked open
  • 1 inch Fresh ginger root, pounded in mortar
  • 1 quill Ceylon cinnamon & 2 whole cloves
The Master's Secret: Simmer the bruised spices in boiling water for 3 full minutes BEFORE adding milk to extract essential oils without scalding.
Master Recipe 02 7 Mins Brew

Imperial Kashmiri Kesar Chai

An opulent celebration simmer featuring saffron bloom extraction, roasted green cardamom, and a touch of raw golden sugar.

Master Blend Elements:
  • 1.5 cups Pure mountain spring water
  • 1.5 cups Rich whole farm milk
  • 8-10 strands Grade-A Kashmiri Mongra Saffron
  • 2 tbsp Single-estate orthodox black tea
  • 3 pods Green cardamom & 1 clove
  • 1 tbsp Raw golden unrefined jaggery/sugar
The Master's Secret: Bloom saffron strands in 2 tablespoons of warm milk for 10 minutes prior to blending to achieve deep sunset golden liquor.
Master Recipe 03 5 Mins Drip

Traditional Malabar Brass Filter Kaapi

The centuries-old South Indian morning ritual brewed through two-tier brass gravity filters and pulled into frothing scalding milk.

Master Blend Elements:
  • 3 tbsp Dark roast Chikmagalur coffee + 15% chicory
  • 3/4 cup Scalding water poured through brass damper
  • 3/4 cup Boiling whole milk (unadulterated)
  • 1-2 tsp Desi cane sugar to taste
  • Serve In traditional brass Davara and Dabarah tumbler
The Master's Secret: Pour the brew back and forth between the tumbler and davara from a meter high ("the pull") to build rich microfoam without steam wands.
